# Problem 1: Recognizing Four Conics

## Problem

Identify each conic and its main parameters:

**(a)** $x^2+y^2=9$.

**(b)** $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$.

**(c)** $\dfrac{x^2}{4}-y^2=1$.

**(d)** $y^2=8x$.

## Worked Solution

### (a) Circle

The equation is $x^2+y^2=3^2$, so the centre is $(0,0)$ and the radius is $3$. The circle has intercepts $(\pm3,0)$ and $(0,\pm3)$.

### (b) Ellipse

The canonical equation has $a^2=9$ and $b^2=4$, with the larger denominator under $x^2$. The centre is $(0,0)$, the semiaxes are $a=3$ horizontally and $b=2$ vertically, and the vertices are $(\pm3,0)$ and $(0,\pm2)$. Since $c^2=a^2-b^2=5$, the foci are $(\pm\sqrt5,0)$ and the eccentricity is $\sqrt5/3$.

### (c) Hyperbola

Rewrite as $x^2/2^2-y^2/1^2=1$. Its centre is $(0,0)$, its transverse axis is horizontal, $a=2$, and $b=1$. The vertices are $(\pm2,0)$; $c^2=a^2+b^2=5$, so the foci are $(\pm\sqrt5,0)$. Its asymptotes are $y=\pm(b/a)x=\pm x/2$.

### (d) Parabola

Compare $y^2=8x$ with $y^2=4px$. Then $4p=8$, so $p=2$. The vertex is $(0,0)$, the axis is the positive $x$-axis, the parabola opens right, the focus is $(2,0)$, and the directrix is $x=-2$.

**Check.** Each classification follows from its canonical form: equal positive squared terms give a circle, unequal positive terms an ellipse, opposite signs a hyperbola, and one squared variable a parabola.
