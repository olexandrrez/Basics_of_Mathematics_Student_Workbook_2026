# Problem 5: Vertex, Focus, and Directrix of a Parabola

## Problem

Find the vertex, focus, and directrix of
$$ (y-1)^2=12(x+2).$$

## Worked Solution

The standard right-opening form is $(y-k)^2=4p(x-h)$. Matching terms gives $h=-2$, $k=1$, and $4p=12$, so $p=3$. Since $p>0$, the parabola opens to the right.

The vertex is $(h,k)=(-2,1)$. The focus is $p$ units to the right, $(h+p,k)=(1,1)$, and the directrix is $p$ units to the left, $x=h-p=-5$.

**Answer:** vertex $\boxed{(-2,1)}$, focus $\boxed{(1,1)}$, directrix $\boxed{x=-5}$.

**Check.** The focus and directrix are each three units from the vertex along the horizontal axis, as required by $p=3$.
