# Problem 3: Canonical Form, Semiaxes, and Foci

## Problem

Put
$$4x^2+9y^2-8x+36y+4=0$$
into canonical form. State its centre, semiaxes, and foci.

## Worked Solution

Group by variable and complete the squares, keeping the coefficients with their terms:
$$4(x^2-2x)+9(y^2+4y)+4=0,$$
$$4\big((x-1)^2-1\big)+9\big((y+2)^2-4\big)+4=0.$$
After combining constants, this becomes
$$4(x-1)^2+9(y+2)^2=36.$$
Divide by $36$ to obtain the canonical form
$$\boxed{\frac{(x-1)^2}{9}+\frac{(y+2)^2}{4}=1}.$$
The centre is $(1,-2)$. The horizontal semiaxis is $a=3$ and the vertical semiaxis is $b=2$, so the major axis is horizontal. For an ellipse, $c^2=a^2-b^2=9-4=5$; the foci are
$$\boxed{(1-\sqrt5,-2)\quad\text{and}\quad(1+\sqrt5,-2)}.$$

**Check.** The semiaxis squares satisfy $c^2+b^2=5+4=9=a^2$, as required for an ellipse.
