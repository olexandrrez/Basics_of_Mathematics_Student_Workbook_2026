# Problem 6: Circle Through Three Points

## Problem

Find the circle through $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$, and $(0,2)$.

## Worked Solution

Write a general circle as $x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0$. Substituting $(0,0)$ gives $F=0$. Substituting $(4,0)$ gives $16+4D=0$, so $D=-4$. Substituting $(0,2)$ gives $4+2E=0$, so $E=-2$.

Therefore the circle is
$$x^2+y^2-4x-2y=0.$$
Complete the squares to identify its centre and radius:
$$ (x-2)^2-4+(y-1)^2-1=0,$$
$$\boxed{(x-2)^2+(y-1)^2=5}.$$
Its centre is $(2,1)$ and its radius is $\sqrt5$.

**Check.** Substitution of the three given points into the general equation gives zero in each case.
