# Problem 2: Centre and Radius of a Circle

## Problem

Find the centre and radius of
$$x^2+y^2-6x+4y-12=0,$$
and write its canonical form.

## Worked Solution

Group the $x$- and $y$-terms and complete each square:
$$x^2-6x+y^2+4y=12,$$
$$ (x-3)^2-9+(y+2)^2-4=12.$$
Move the constants to the right:
$$ (x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=25.$$
Comparing with $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$ gives centre $(h,k)=(3,-2)$ and radius $r=5$.

**Answer:** canonical form $\boxed{(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=25}$; centre $\boxed{(3,-2)}$; radius $\boxed5$.

**Check.** Expanding the canonical form gives $x^2+y^2-6x+4y-12=0$.
