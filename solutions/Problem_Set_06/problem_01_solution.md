# Problem 1: Plane from a Point and a Normal

## Problem

Find a Cartesian equation of the plane through $P=(1,0,-1)$ with normal $n=(2,-1,3)$.

## Worked Solution

A plane with normal $n$ through $P$ consists of points whose displacement from $P$ is perpendicular to $n$. Thus
$$n\cdot((x,y,z)-P)=0.$$
Substituting the data gives
$$2(x-1)-(y-0)+3(z+1)=0.$$
After expanding and combining constants,
$$\boxed{2x-y+3z+1=0}.$$

**Check.** Substituting $P$ gives $2(1)-0+3(-1)+1=0$, and the coefficients of $x,y,z$ are the given normal components.
