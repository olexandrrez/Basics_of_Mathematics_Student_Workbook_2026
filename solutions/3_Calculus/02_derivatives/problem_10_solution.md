# Problem 10: The Tangent Line

## 1. Original Problem
For $f(x)=x^2-3x+1$, find the tangent at $x=2$, state the point and slope, and verify incidence.

## 2. What We Are Trying to Find
A tangent line is determined by its contact point and slope. We obtain the point from the function and the slope from the derivative.

## 3. Key Idea or Rule Needed Before the Calculation
At $x=a$, the point is $(a,f(a))$ and slope is $f'(a)$. A line through $(x_0,y_0)$ with slope $m$ has equation $y-y_0=m(x-x_0)$.

## 4. Step-by-Step Solution
Evaluate the function: $f(2)=4-6+1=-1$, so the point is $(2,-1)$. Differentiate: $f'(x)=2x-3$, hence $f'(2)=1$. Substitute the point and slope into point-slope form:
$$y+1=x-2,$$
which simplifies to $y=x-3$.

## 5. Why Each Step Is Valid
The derivative gives the instantaneous rate of change, which is the tangent slope. Point-slope form gives the unique line through the contact point with that slope.

## 6. Final Answer in Words
The tangent point is $(2,-1)$, the slope is 1, and the tangent line is $y=x-3$.

## 7. Quick Verification
At $x=2$, the line yields $y=-1$, matching the curve. Its slope is 1, matching $f'(2)$.

## 8. Common Mistakes
- Using $f(2)$ as the slope rather than $f'(2)$.
- Forgetting the point's $y$-coordinate when writing point-slope form.

## 9. Short In-Class Explanation
Evaluate the curve at 2 to get $(2,-1)$, then differentiate and evaluate to get slope 1. The point-slope formula gives $y=x-3$, which passes through the point.
