# Problem 7: Acute Angle Between Two Lines

## Problem

Find the acute angle between $y=x+1$ and $y=3x-2$.

## Worked Solution

The slopes are $m_1=1$ and $m_2=3$. For two nonvertical lines, the tangent of the acute angle between them is
$$\tan\theta=\left|\frac{m_2-m_1}{1+m_1m_2}\right|.$$
Substitute the slopes:
$$\tan\theta=\left|\frac{3-1}{1+3}\right|=\frac12.$$
Therefore
$$\boxed{\theta=\arctan(1/2)\approx26.565^\circ}.$$

**Check.** The slopes differ, so the lines are not parallel; the acute angle is positive and less than $90^\circ$, consistent with this value.
