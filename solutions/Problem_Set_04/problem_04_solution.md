# Problem 4: A Parallel Line

## Problem

Find the equation of the line through $P=(4,-3)$ parallel to $5x-2y+7=0$. Give the result in general and slope-intercept forms. Calculate where the new line intersects the $x$-axis.

## Worked Solution

Parallel lines have proportional normal vectors. The given line has normal vector $(5,-2)$, so a parallel line has form $5x-2y+C=0$. Substitute $P=(4,-3)$ to determine $C$:
$$5(4)-2(-3)+C=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad26+C=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad C=-26.$$
The general equation is $\boxed{5x-2y-26=0}$. Solving for $y$ gives the slope-intercept form
$$\boxed{y=\frac52x-13}.$$
At the $x$-axis, $y=0$, so $5x-26=0$ and $x=26/5$.

**Answer:** the line intersects the $x$-axis at $\boxed{(26/5,0)}$.

**Check.** The line passes through $(4,-3)$, and its coefficients of $x$ and $y$ match the original line, confirming parallelism.
